Visa & requirements

A German national (long-stay) student visa generally requires university admission, proof of financial resources (often via a blocked account), health insurance, and, for many programs, a German language certificate — which is what our German Language Training prepares you for.

Current visa fees and blocked-account thresholds are set by German authorities and change periodically — confirm the exact requirements with a Studies & Awards counsellor. Talk to a counsellor
