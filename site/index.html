GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Do you offer IELTS and PTE classes?+

Yes. Our trainers teach IELTS and PTE in person and online, and we can also register you for the exam.

Can you help me with my visa?+

Yes. We guide you through the visa application, including medical insurance, medical appointments and biometrics appointments.

Which countries can I study in?+

Australia, New Zealand, Ireland and Germany, as well as the United Kingdom and Canada. A counsellor will help you choose.

Do you help with flights?+

Yes. Through our partnership with Reserve Trip Kenya, our students can get discounted student air tickets.

Can I get help with school fees?+

Ask us about financial support through Magister Sacco.

Where is your office?+

Daima Towers, Mezzanine 1, Eldoret, adjacent to Marie Stopes Eldoret.

Ready to start your journey abroad?

Call us or visit our office at Daima Towers, Eldoret.
