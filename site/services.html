OUR SERVICES

One team with you from your first meeting to your visa.

Every student goes through the same six steps with us, whichever of our six countries they choose. Each step has its own team in our office at Daima Towers, Eldoret, so you always know who is looking after you.

    Step 1 · Start here

    Free consultation

    Visit our office at Daima Towers in Eldoret, or meet our team online from anywhere in Kenya. We talk through your grades, budget and goals, and help you choose the country, course and school that suit you. Every member of our team gives consultations, so whoever you meet first can get you started.

    • Your grades, budget and goals
    • The countries, courses and schools that fit them
    • Whether you have already sat IELTS elsewhere. If you have the score you need, you go straight to step 3.

    Who you’ll meet

    Any member of our team. Meet the team

    Step 2

    Language classes

    Witney prepares you for your English test, IELTS or PTE. If you are going to Germany, Karen teaches you German. Either way, you choose how you learn.

    • In classAt Daima Towers, during the day
    • OnlineIn the evening, from wherever you are

    When you are ready, we book your exam for you. If you don’t reach the score you need, you come back to class and we book your next attempt.

      Step 3

      Documentation

      Rahab tells you exactly which documents your application needs, and keeps them safely on file once they are in. When your documents are complete and approved, she passes your file on to our compliance team.

      • Each document certified by a lawyer
      • Each one clearly scanned
      • Uploaded to us securely

      • 4

        Step 4

        Verification and compliance

        Canisius and Dennis check that every document you have given us is authentic, by confirming it with whoever issued it. Only once everything has been checked does your file move on to the application team.

        • Certificates confirmed with the school or college that issued them
        • Bank statements confirmed with the bank
        • Every document checked before anything goes to a school

        Who looks after you

          Ask about verification
        • 5

          Step 5

          Application and offer

          1. Choosing and applying

            Joy helps you settle on the course and school that best match what you want. Once you agree, she applies for you and follows up until your offer letter arrives, conditional or unconditional.

          2. Meeting the offer’s conditions

            Ian makes sure every condition on your offer is met, including the documents the school asks for before it approves you (its GTE requirements). If the school asks for a statement of purpose (GSR) explaining why you want to study there, Beatrice and the GSR team write it for you. Ian then sends everything back to the school.

          3. Fees and confirmation of enrolment

            When the school approves you, Winnie, our application manager, makes sure your fees are paid and your confirmation of enrolment (CoE) is issued. Winnie oversees every application from start to finish.

            Ask about applications

            Step 6

            Visa and travel

            1. Your visa

              Mourine and Joyner take care of every part of your visa application, from your health cover to your biometrics.

              • Health cover arranged
              • Medical examination booked
              • Visa data entry: your application form filled in for you
              • Visa audit: the whole application checked, your statement of purpose included, before it is submitted
              • Biometrics appointment booked

            2. Your flight

              Once your visa is granted, Mourine books your flight at a discounted student fare.
