Visa & requirements

Studying in New Zealand for more than three months generally requires a student visa, an offer of place from an approved education provider, evidence of funds for tuition and living costs, health and character checks, and an accepted English test score where the provider asks for one.

Current visa fees and financial-evidence thresholds are set by Immigration New Zealand and change from time to time.
