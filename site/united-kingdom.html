Visa & requirements

Studying in the UK generally requires a Student visa, an offer from a licensed student sponsor, proof of financial means to cover tuition and living costs, and an accepted English test score.

Current visa fees, financial-evidence thresholds and the Immigration Health Surcharge are set by UK Visas and Immigration and change from time to time.
