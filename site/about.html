ABOUT US

Helping Kenyan students reach world-class classrooms.

Studies and Awards Limited is an education consultancy based in Eldoret, connecting students to higher education opportunities abroad through trusted university partnerships.

Our Vision

“We envision a future where every Kenyan student has the opportunity to pursue international education, supported by expert guidance and tailored solutions that enable them to thrive academically and professionally on a global scale.”

Our Mission

“To facilitate access to quality education abroad for Kenyan students, ensuring affordability, transparency, and personalised support throughout their academic journey.”

STRATEGIC GOALS

What guides how we work

Access & Affordability

Removing financial barriers to international education for students from varied socioeconomic backgrounds.

Continuous Improvement

Maintaining current practice and adopting emerging technologies and methodologies in our services.

Fraud-Free Operations

Strict verification protocols that protect students and institutional partners throughout the process.
