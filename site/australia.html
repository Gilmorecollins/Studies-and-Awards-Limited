Visa & requirements

You apply for a Student visa (subclass 500) once your education provider issues your Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE). You must also meet the Genuine Student requirement, hold Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC) for your whole stay, show you can pay for your tuition and living costs, and give an accepted English test result such as IELTS or PTE.

Current visa subclass, fees and financial thresholds are set by the Australian government and change from time to time.
