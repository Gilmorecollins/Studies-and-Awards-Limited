Visa & requirements

Studying in Australia generally requires a student visa, proof of enrolment, evidence of financial capacity, overseas student health cover, and an accepted English test score such as IELTS or PTE.

Current visa subclass, fees and financial thresholds are set by the Australian government and change periodically — confirm the exact requirements with a Studies & Awards counsellor. Talk to a counsellor
