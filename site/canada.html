Visa & requirements

A Canadian study permit generally requires a letter of acceptance from a designated learning institution, proof of financial support, and a medical exam where applicable.

Current study permit fees and financial-proof thresholds are set by Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada and change from time to time.
